# Learning topics without a journey

Accepted presentation: remove the castle journey, named posts, rank emblems,
unlocks and arrival/completion story screens. See
[Knightly direction](../knightly-direction.md).

The backend's existing teaching sequence remains valid. Its ward, lesson,
challenge, answer and completion identifiers remain unchanged so this UI update
does not reset progress or invalidate saved sessions. Present served lessons by
their chess topic and challenges as practice checks, without place names or
ranks. Lesson introductions still explain the chess idea on the plain board.
Future curriculum work belongs to [curriculum](2026-10-20-curriculum-wards-design.md),
not to a game map or an unlock presentation.

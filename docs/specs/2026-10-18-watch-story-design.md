# Practice presentation

Accepted. The app uses plain chess language and direct learning activities, as
specified in [Knightly direction](../knightly-direction.md) and the
[daily practice home](2026-10-19-path-home-design.md).

Practice and review retain the segmented progress bar, points with their star
icon, brief reward feedback, hints, move grades and explanations. Lessons use
the normal fitted-board layout with a plain background. A character-free panel
shows instructions and feedback without a speech-bubble tail. No knight,
castle, wall, raider or landscape is part of the learning screen.

Say practice/session, threats, completed and correct answers. Session results
and the streak acknowledgement remain; castle, post and rank celebrations do
not. Reminder time and habit anchoring remain account features with plain
practice wording. Internal API names may still use `watch`, `ward` and journey
fields for server compatibility; these names do not prescribe product copy.

Development art galleries retain original artwork and provenance separately
from production navigation.

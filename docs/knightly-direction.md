# Knightly direction

Accepted: focus on the chess learning loop. Review your own games, practice
recurring mistakes, and apply what you learn in future games.

## Product presentation

Keep the segmented lesson progress bar, streak and calendar, points counter,
star icon, session score breakdown and brief reward feedback. Keep meaningful
move grades, hints, explanations and spaced-repetition results. Preserve the
existing scoring, scheduling, saved learning history and account isolation.

Remove the knight characters, castle scenery, journey map, ranks, post unlocks
and story celebrations from the app. Home offers the daily practice session,
game review, Games and Progress directly. Progress shows chess improvement,
practice consistency and memory without rank or star progression.

Practice and review use a plain background and a character-free explanation
panel. Session completion shows results and the streak acknowledgement when
applicable. Use plain language: practice, review, threats, correct answers and
practice schedule. Do not call sessions watches, threats raids, or completion
holding a post. Account reminder settings retain their time and habit features.

The Knightly name, chess-piece artwork, brand mark, dark palette, typography and
buttons remain. The reward star is a score icon, not a character or a rank.
Development art galleries are not product navigation or learning progression.
